About us

Clinical Excellence. Compassionate Care. Community Commitment.

Lumina Hospital is a fully accredited private healthcare institution dedicated to delivering world-class, affordable medical services to the people of Ado-Ekiti and its environs. We bridge the gap between advanced medical technology and accessible healthcare—serving civil servants, traders, students, and families alike.

At Lumina Hospital, we restore wellness with professionalism and compassion—contributing meaningfully to the health transformation of Ado-Ekiti and beyond.

Our specializations

Six departments, one standard of care

General Medicine

Primary care, built around you

From routine checkups to managing chronic conditions, our primary care physicians get to know you as a person, not just a chart. We coordinate everything—labs, referrals, follow-ups—so your care never falls through the cracks. Preventive screening, vaccinations, and personalized health guidance keep small issues from becoming big ones.

Behavioural Health

Support for the mind, not just the body

Our licensed therapists and psychiatrists create a calm, judgment-free space to work through anxiety, depression, grief, and everyday stress. Sessions are private and paced to you, whether that means weekly talk therapy, medication management, or a one-time consultation. Healing takes time, and we stay with you through it.

Laboratory & Imaging

Answers you can trust, fast

Same-day bloodwork, ultrasound, X-ray, and CT services mean fewer days spent waiting and wondering. Our lab is calibrated and cross-checked against international standards, and every result is reviewed by a physician before it reaches you—so you get clarity, not just numbers.

Bone & Joint

Getting you moving again

Whether it's a sports injury, arthritis, or a joint that's been aching for years, our orthopedic team builds a plan around your mobility goals. We lean on physiotherapy and minimally invasive techniques first, reserving surgery for when it's truly the better option.

Child & Adolescent Health

Care that grows with your child

From newborn checkups to teenage growth spurts, our pediatric team tracks development milestones and keeps immunizations on schedule. We talk to kids at their level and to parents in plain language, so nobody leaves an appointment with unanswered questions.

Heart & Vascular

Protecting the heart of the matter

Our cardiologists combine advanced diagnostics—ECG, echocardiography, stress testing—with honest conversations about lifestyle and risk. Whether you're managing hypertension or recovering from a cardiac event, we build a treatment plan that fits your life, not just your chart.

Our mission

Care that puts people first.

We make excellent healthcare feel more human, more accessible, and easier to understand for every family we serve.

Our clinicians pair modern diagnostics and treatment with clear communication, so patients in Ado-Ekiti and surrounding communities can make informed decisions about their health.
