Our procedure

What happens when you arrive

Knowing the next step can make a difficult moment feel more manageable. Our teams prioritize immediate danger first while keeping families informed.

01

Call or arrive

Call the emergency desk, or come directly to our emergency entrance. Ambulance arrivals are received by the clinical team.

02

Rapid assessment

A nurse quickly checks the patient’s condition and identifies anything that needs immediate attention.

03

Urgent treatment

The appropriate doctor and support team begin care, tests, monitoring, or stabilization without unnecessary delay.

04

Next steps

We explain the care plan, prescriptions, referrals, admission, or safe discharge instructions before you leave.

Before you come

Bring what helps us help you

  • A list of current medicines and allergies
  • Identification and insurance information, if available
  • A trusted contact person and relevant medical history
  • Comfort items for a child, when appropriate
